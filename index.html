Meet the founder

Photo of BJ

BJ Hanley

Founder · Hitting Instructor

BJ grew up in Northern California and earned a baseball scholarship to Southern Virginia University, where he finished his career as the school’s record holder in home runs, RBI and batting average. Now based in Spotsylvania, he coaches hitters of every age.

  • Southern Virginia University — played college baseball on scholarship
  • SVU career record holder — home runs, RBI and batting average
  • 2014 District 15 Champions — coached the SCLL District All-Star team to the title
  • Hitting lessons for every age — based in Spotsylvania, Virginia
