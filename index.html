About the coach

BJ Hanley

Owner, Hanley Hitting LLC

BJ runs Hanley Hitting out of the Lab, the indoor training facility he built in Fredericksburg, Virginia. He gives private and open hitting lessons and runs the Cardinals travel baseball teams.

The easiest way to reach him is a Facebook message. That’s where he books lessons and posts open slots.
